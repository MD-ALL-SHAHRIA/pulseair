# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Criticise work, not the person who wrote it.

## What's expected

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements here will be about methodology, and the person
  you disagree with is usually trying to get the same thing right that you are.
- **Challenge results directly.** This project reports negative findings on purpose, and
  some of them may be wrong. Saying "this analysis is flawed, here's why" is welcome and
  is not rudeness. Saying "you're an idiot" is not the same thing.
- Bring evidence. In a methodology dispute, a re-run or a citation settles it; volume
  doesn't.
- Accept that maintainers may say no. This repository backs a specific thesis, and not
  every good idea belongs in it.

## What's not

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, deliberate intimidation, posting
others' private information, and sustained disruption after being asked to stop.

## Scope

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, and anywhere else
someone is representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening a private security advisory (which is the private channel
available on this repo) or by contacting a maintainer directly. Reports will be handled
confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block accounts
when this document is violated, and will say plainly when they have.
